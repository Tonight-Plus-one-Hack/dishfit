DishFit How it works

An AI pipeline reads every menu.
We show how sure it is.

01 findRestaurants

OpenStreetMap per Kiez, Google Places only to filter open venues (we store just the place id).

02 menuOwn website

HTML and PDF menus, robots.txt respected, no delivery apps. Claude extracts dishes and prices.

03 nutritionIngredients × USDA

The model lists ingredients and grams; code computes macros from USDA data. Official chain values win.

04 checkSecond opinion

Outliers get re-estimated by a second model; disagreement widens the range and drops confidence.

05 reviewTeam and community

The team reviews each Kiez. Users can submit menu photos to the review queue.

Nutrition benchmark
We measured 15 % mean absolute kcal error against official values. Blind AI estimates had 19 % error.
  • Every AI number is a range (±15 / 25 / 35 % by confidence) with a "KI-Schätzung" label; 10 of 16 official values fall inside it.
  • Protein still runs ~8 g high on chicken dishes, so rankings use the low end of the range.
  • Official chain values replace estimates wherever they exist.
