# QR poster copy

Draft only.
Nothing is printed or put up until the captain says go, and every poster needs the venue's permission first.

## Brand look

Every poster uses the Plus One brand kit in `brand/` (tokens in `docs/design/tokens.json`).

- Canvas: off-white `#fcfcfd`; text in ink `#1c2024`, secondary text in muted `#60646c`.
- Headline: Source Serif 4, regular weight, tight tracking (-0.04em).
- Body: Geist; small labels such as the URL line: Geist Mono.
  Fonts are bundled in `brand/fonts/`.
- Logo: `brand/assets/logo-primary.svg` at the top, with half a symbol-width of clear space around it.
  Never redraw or recolor the logo.
- Artwork: `brand/assets/city-berlin.webp` as a band above the QR, with 16 px rounded corners (scaled to print).
- QR: ink on white, generated by `make-qr.mjs` (its defaults already use the brand ink).
- No shadows, thin 1 px rules in `#d9d9e0` if a divider is needed.

## Format

- A4 or A5 portrait, high contrast, large QR in the lower half.
- QR at least 4 cm wide for A5, 6 cm for A4, with the white margin kept.
- Generate each QR with `--ecc Q` so it still scans when a corner gets scuffed.
- One `utm_content` per location, so we know which poster works.
- Below the QR, print the short URL in plain text for people whose camera won't scan.

```sh
node launch/make-qr.mjs "{APP_URL}/berlin" --source qr --medium poster --campaign launch-berlin --content hostel-1 --ecc Q --out /tmp/wohin-qr/hostel-1
```

Keep a simple list of which `utm_content` id is on which wall (location, date, who put it up).

## Asking for permission at the venue

EN:

> Hi, we're building a small app that helps people who are on their own in Berlin find a group to go out with.
> Would it be okay to put this poster up near reception?
> It's free, no ads for other businesses, and you can take it down any time.

DE:

> Hallo, wir bauen eine kleine App, mit der Leute, die allein in Berlin sind, eine Runde zum Ausgehen finden.
> Dürfen wir dieses Plakat in der Nähe der Rezeption aufhängen?
> Es ist kostenlos, keine Werbung für andere Läden, und ihr könnt es jederzeit abnehmen.

If the answer is no, say thanks and leave.
