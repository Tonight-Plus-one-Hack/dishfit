# Outreach templates

Drafts only.
Nobody is contacted until the captain says go.
All names are placeholders; never paste a real person's name into this file.

Rules for every message:

- One message per group or person, no follow-up spam.
  One polite follow-up after 24 hours at most, and only if they replied or reacted.
- Ask the group admin before posting in a group; respect "no promotion" rules.
- Say clearly that we built it at a hackathon this weekend.
- Every link carries the right `utm_source` (see `ads.md`).

## Hostel receptions (`utm_source=qr`)

Hostels get a poster with its own `utm_content` id (see `qr-poster.md`), so the source is `qr`, not a DM.

### In person (EN)

Hi, we're building a small app at a hackathon this weekend that helps travelers on their own find a group to go out with tonight.
Would you be okay with us putting a small poster near reception, or could you mention it to guests who ask where to go tonight?
It's free, it only lists public bars and clubs, and you can take it down any time.

### In person (DE)

Hallo, wir bauen dieses Wochenende auf einem Hackathon eine kleine App, mit der Reisende, die allein unterwegs sind, eine Runde zum Ausgehen finden.
Wäre es okay, wenn wir ein kleines Plakat an der Rezeption aufhängen, oder könntet ihr es Gästen empfehlen, die fragen, wo man heute hingehen kann?
Es ist kostenlos, zeigt nur öffentliche Bars und Clubs, und ihr könnt es jederzeit abnehmen.
