## 4-minute version (about 560 words)

**Opening, about 30 s, slide 1 showing**

Raise your hand if you have ever arrived in a new city and spent the first evening alone in your room, scrolling.
That is most travelers, most Erasmus students, most people in their first week at a new job.
Going out alone feels weird.
Asking strangers on the internet feels unsafe.
So people stay in.

**Product, about 60 s, slide 1**

Plus One is a Luma-style city app for exactly that night.
You pick a city, like you would on Luma.
The city page shows tonight: open hangouts with a time, a public spot, a vibe like bar, club, chill or culture, the language, and how many seats are left.
Every hangout is a small group, three to eight people, at a place from our curated list of public spots.
Joining is one question per screen: a nickname, your mood, and an 18+ confirmation, and you are in the group chat.
When you get there, you tap "I'm here".

If nothing fits, you start your own hangout in about twenty seconds: pick a spot, a time, a vibe and a size.
Then you share the link, because a hangout needs people.
That is the growth loop: every new hangout is a reason to share, and there is a "bring a friend" seat.

Each city also has a spots page: a map with pins and large cards for the best public places to meet, from OpenStreetMap data, filtered by vibe.

**Why it matters, about 45 s, slide 3**

For Layla and Expedia, the first night out is the moment a traveler decides how they feel about a city.
Plus One owns that moment, and then hands them to Layla to plan the rest of the trip, with stays on Expedia, Hotels.com and Vrbo.
New-in-town people are exactly the people who book the next stay.

Business model, still ideas: commission on stays booked through the hand-off, Layla as the trip planner after the first night, venue partners that are always labeled as promoted, and hostels and student networks as city partners.

**Safety, about 30 s, slide 3**

Meeting strangers only works if it is safe by design.
Groups only, never one-on-one.
Public spots from a curated list only, no private addresses and no free-text locations.
Report and block on every hangout and every message: a message is hidden after two reports, a hangout after three, and our team reviews reports in an admin view.
18+ confirmation, safety hints on every hangout page, hangouts expire at 6 am, and no location tracking at all: you choose the city, we never locate you.
